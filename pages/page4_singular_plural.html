Singular (One) vs Plural (Two or More)!

Use a singular noun for one person/thing. Add -s to make most nouns plural! Use a before consonants and an before vowels (a, e, i, o, u).

🅰️ Articles Helper: "A" vs "AN"

📌 "an" is used before vowels (a, e, i, o, u). "a" is used before consonants!

Use "AN" (Vowels):

🎨 an artist 👁️ an eye 🐝 an insect 🍳 an oven ☂️ an umbrella

Use "A" (Consonants):

🌊 a river 🏰 a castle 🏞️ a park 👨‍⚕️ a doctor
