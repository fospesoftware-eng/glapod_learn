🪞 Reflexive Pronouns Mirror Studio!

Reflexive pronouns refer back to the subject of the verb: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves!

📜 Pronoun Matching Reference

I (subject) myself
you (singular) yourself
he (subject) himself
she (subject) herself
it (subject) itself
we (subject) ourselves
they (subject) themselves
