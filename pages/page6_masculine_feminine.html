🤴 Masculine (Male) & 👸 Feminine (Female) Nouns

Masculine nouns are words for men, boys, and male animals. Feminine nouns are words for women, girls, and female animals!

👥 People Gender Pairs

👦 boy ↔️ 👧 girl
👑 king ↔️ 👸 queen
🤴 prince ↔️ 👑 princess
🧙‍♂️ wizard ↔️ 🧙‍♀️ witch
👨‍🍳 waiter ↔️ 👩‍🍳 waitress
👴 grandfather ↔️ 👵 grandmother

🐾 Animal Gender Pairs

🐴 stallion (male) ↔️ 🐴 mare (female)
🐓 rooster (male) ↔️ 🐔 hen (female)
🐂 bull (male) ↔️ 🐄 cow (female)
🦁 lion (male) ↔️ 🦁 lioness (female)
🦊 fox (male) ↔️ 🦊 vixen (female)
🐑 ram (male) ↔️ 🐑 ewe (female)
