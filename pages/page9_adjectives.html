🎨 Kinds of Adjectives & Comparison Transformer!

Adjectives describe nouns! Compare two things using Comparative (-er) and three or more using Superlative (-est)!

🌈 Kinds of Adjectives

📏 Size:

big tiny huge tall short

🎨 Color:

red blue green yellow purple

⭐ Quality:

beautiful kind sunny cool sweet

🪵 Substance (What it's made of):

wooden plastic golden silk glass
